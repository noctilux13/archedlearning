# Art & Architecture History Learning Platform (Politecnico di milano architeceure ARCHED test knowledge platform)

A modern, minimalist, and interactive learning platform covering Western Art History and Classical European Architecture movements, master artists/architects, authoritative artworks, 3D flashcards, and an AI study assistant.

## ✨ Features

- **Panoramic Movements**: Comprehensive coverage from Baroque, Neoclassicism, Industrial Architecture, Art Nouveau to Expressionism, Surrealism, and Contemporary Art.
- **English-First Academic Hierarchy**: English-first typography with accurate Chinese translations and historical context.
- **3D Physics Flashcards**: Keyboard-navigable (Space / Arrow keys) memory flashcards with 3D spring flip physics.
- **Curated Knowledge Checklist & Quiz**: Real exam-style questions with instant feedback and AI-generated dynamic quizzes.
- **High-Res Lightbox**: Full-screen lossless inspection for master paintings and architectural landmarks.
- **AI Tutor & Study Diagnosis**: Integrated Groq AI academic deep dives and progress analysis.
